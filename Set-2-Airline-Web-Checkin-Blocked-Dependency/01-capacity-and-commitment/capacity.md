# Capacity and commitment

**Question:** Compute the team capacity (six members, 8 working days, 70 percent focus factor) and decide the number of points to commit.

### Team capacity

| | |
|---|---|
| Team members | 6 |
| Working days in the sprint | 8 (2 public holidays in this 10-day sprint) |
| Hours per day | 8 |
| Focus factor | 70% (meetings, support, interruptions) |
| **Capacity** | 6 × 8 × 8 × 0.70 = **268.8 hours** |

### Points to commit

Last three sprints (10 working days each): velocity 29, 31, 30 → average **30 points** for 6 × 10 × 8 × 0.7 = 336 focus hours.

Points per focus hour = 30 / 336 = 0.089 → 268.8 × 0.089 = **24 points**.
(Same as scaling by days: 30 × 8 / 10 = 24.)

The team commits to **24 points** and keeps a 3-point story ready as a stretch item.
